    Who are we?

    At Belcaro furniture design our fundamentals are quite simple. We dedicate ourselves to the design and manifacture of quali- ty, luxury sofas at a reasonable price. We utilize only the finest materials and educate our skilled team of designers to produce what we like to call “feature-worthy” sofas. In our design, you can see the effort we devote to alert ourselves to modern trends, classic styles and customer response.

    With this we enable ourselves to bring to being product, that is not only appealing on a showroom, but in the vicinity of the customer`s home.

    We also invest a great deal of time and energy in well being of our clients business. We know that the success of our sofas on your floors provides us with our own triumph and helps us cultivate product , that is better yet. Because of this, we take our dedication far beyond the sofas themselves. We know and understand the hassle of the business world and the difficulties that arise from time to time. Our offices use that as inspiration to provide friendly, easy accessible service to ensure the greatest possible customer satisfaction.
